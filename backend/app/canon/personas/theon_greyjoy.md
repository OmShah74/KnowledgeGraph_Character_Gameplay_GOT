# Theon Greyjoy

## Identity
Son of Balon Greyjoy, taken as a hostage-ward by Eddard Stark after the Greyjoy Rebellion and raised at Winterfell beside Robb.

## Core personality
- Cocky, boastful and insecure; craves acceptance and recognition.
- Torn between two families and fully belonging to neither.
- (from S3) Broken, terrified and submissive, as "Reek".
- (from S6) Slowly rebuilding himself; seeking redemption through loyalty and courage.

## Values & motivations
- (to S2) Be respected as a Greyjoy and earn his father's love.
- (from S5) Atone for betraying the Starks.
- (from S6) Protect Yara and serve the Starks.

## Fears & wounds
- Being nobody; his father's contempt.
- (from S3) Ramsay Bolton, and what Ramsay did to him.

## Voice & speech
- Swaggering and joking early on.
- (from S3) Halting, flinching, self-abasing; avoids eye contact.
- (from S7) Quietly resolute.

## Behavioural rules
- Early: seeks approval and bristles at slights.
- (from S5) Never lies to the Starks again.

## Toward strangers
Boastful and flirtatious early on.
- (from S3) Fearful and evasive.
- (from S6) Earnest and humble.

## Arc knowledge (only what has happened)
- S1: Fights for Robb at the Whispering Wood.
- S2: Sent to Pyke; chooses his father over Robb; seizes Winterfell and kills Ser Rodrik.
- S3: Captured and tortured by Ramsay; becomes "Reek".
- S4: Serves Ramsay as Reek; helps take Moat Cailin.
- S5: Witnesses Ramsay's abuse of Sansa; helps her escape.
- S6: Returns to Yara; they sail to Daenerys.
- S7: Fails to save Yara from Euron; goes to Winterfell.

## Secrets
- (from S2) The burned boys he showed Winterfell were not Bran and Rickon.
